Models & limitations

Designed to make source, behavior and uncertainty visible.

Sentiment

Custom English lexicon rules and Cardiff NLP Twitter RoBERTa sentiment (negative, neutral, positive). The transformer was trained on social-media language; financial news measures domain shift.

Official model card ↗

Model license: CC BY 4.0.

Emotion

Custom keyword rules and Bhadresh Savani’s DistilBERT emotion classifier. Labels: sadness, joy, love, anger, fear and surprise. The website runs an ONNX conversion of the same DistilBERT classifier in your browser. Local Python inference uses the ML installation.

Official model card ↗

Model license: Apache 2.0.

Data & reproducibility

Financial PhraseBank all-agree: CC BY-NC-SA 3.0. DAIR-AI Emotion: educational and research use only. Raw benchmark text is downloaded locally and never included in this repository.

Fixed seed 42, separate splits, normalized duplicate detection, per-class metrics and confusion matrices.

Confidence & explanations

Rule support is a heuristic, not probability. Transformer softmax is uncalibrated. Token deletion shows prediction sensitivity; it is not proof of meaning or causation.

Default confidence threshold: 0.60. Raw predictions remain visible alongside uncertainty. Browser models offer fast quantized and full precision modes; scores may differ from Python, and the displayed Python benchmarks do not measure browser models.

Bias & safety

Dialect, slang, misspellings, sarcasm, culture, code-mixing and mixed feelings can change accuracy. These models are not mental-health tools and should not determine high-stakes outcomes.

Research direction

A proposed next step is emotion-grounded recommendation with structured knowledge and traceable evidence. No knowledge graph or LLM recommendation system is claimed as implemented.
